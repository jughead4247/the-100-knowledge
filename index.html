About The 100 Quiz

Think you know The 100? Put your knowledge to the test with this 50-question trivia quiz covering the major characters, locations, clans and events from the series.

Questions cover everything from the Ark and the Grounders to Mount Weather, Praimfaya, Sanctum, the Primes, Bardo and transcendence.

Each question has four possible answers. A correct answer gives you 2 points, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. The maximum possible score is 100.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does The 100 Quiz work?

You will face 50 questions about The 100. Each question has four possible answers. Choose the answer you believe is correct and see how much you remember from the series.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 100 points. Each correct answer is worth 2 points.

What does my score mean?

Your score determines your rank, ranging from a basic Skaikru casualty to Pramheda-level knowledge.

Does this quiz cover all seasons?

Yes. The questions cover events and characters from across the series, including the Ark, Grounders, Mount Weather, Praimfaya, Sanctum and Bardo.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after receiving your result to replay the quiz and try for a higher score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. After completing the quiz, use the Challenge Your Friends button to share your result and see whether your friends can beat your score.
