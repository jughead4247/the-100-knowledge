About The 100 Quiz

The 100 Quiz is a 50-question trivia challenge for fans of The 100. It tests how well you remember the characters, factions, locations, relationships, technology and major events that shaped the series.

The questions cover different stages of the story, beginning with the Ark and the arrival of the 100 on Earth and continuing through the Grounder conflicts, Mount Weather, Praimfaya, Sanctum, Bardo and the final storyline. Some questions focus on major characters and events, while others test smaller details that can be difficult to remember.

Every question has four possible answers. A correct answer earns 2 points, giving the quiz a maximum score of 100. Your final result is displayed as a percentage and places you into a rank based on your performance.

The quiz is designed for entertainment and trivia. It is not an official test created by The CW, Warner Bros. or the creators of The 100.

How The 100 Quiz Works

The quiz contains 50 multiple-choice questions, with four possible choices presented for every question. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct before continuing.

The questions cover a wide range of subjects from the series. You may encounter questions about characters such as Clarke, Bellamy, Octavia, Raven, Murphy and other members of the different groups. Other questions can involve locations, factions, technology, alliances, conflicts and important discoveries.

Each correct answer is worth 2 points. Since there are 50 questions, the highest possible score is 100. Your final score determines your rank, with results ranging from lower-level outcomes to the highest rank of Pramheda.

A high score requires more than remembering the main characters. Details from different seasons can appear, so remembering events from the Ark, Earth, Mount Weather, Sanctum and Bardo can all help.

If you watched the series more than once, you may have an advantage when questions involve minor characters, specific locations or events from earlier seasons.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question will then appear with four possible choices.

Choosing an answer

Read the complete question and all four choices before selecting your answer. Some questions may involve details from different seasons, so consider the wording carefully before making your choice.

Moving between questions

Use the Next button to move forward through the quiz. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and reconsider your answer before submitting.

Completing the quiz

Answer all 50 questions and submit the quiz when you reach the end. Your final score will then be displayed along with your rank.

Try Again

Select TRY AGAIN after receiving your result if you want another attempt. You can use a second attempt to improve your score or see whether you remember questions that challenged you previously.

Challenge Your Friends

After completing the quiz, use CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share the challenge and see whether another The 100 fan can beat your score.

The 100 Quiz FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 50 multiple-choice questions, and each question has four possible answers.

How is the score calculated?

Every correct answer is worth 2 points. With 50 questions, the maximum possible score is 100.

Does the quiz cover all seven seasons?

Yes. The quiz includes material from throughout the series, covering storylines involving the Ark, Grounders, Mount Weather, Praimfaya, Sanctum, Bardo and the final events of the show.

Which characters can appear in the questions?

Questions can involve major and supporting characters from different parts of the series. Characters from the Ark, Grounder clans, Mount Weather, Sanctum and other groups may all be relevant to the quiz.

Does the quiz include locations and factions?

Yes. The questions are not limited to characters. Locations, factions, communities, technology, relationships and important events can also appear.

Is this a survival quiz?

No. This is primarily a knowledge and trivia quiz. Although The 100 contains many survival and post-apocalyptic themes, your result measures how well you remember the television series rather than your ability to survive an actual disaster.

What is the highest rank?

The highest rank used by this quiz is Pramheda. Your rank is determined by your final score.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after receiving your result to restart the quiz and attempt a higher score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use the CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS button after completing the quiz to share the challenge and compare your results with other fans.

Spoiler Warning

This quiz contains spoilers for The 100, including characters, relationships, factions, locations, major discoveries, conflicts and events from different seasons of the series.

If you have not finished watching the show and want to avoid learning important story details, consider completing the relevant episodes before taking the quiz.
