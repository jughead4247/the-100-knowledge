About The 100 Quiz

Think you know The 100? This 50-question knowledge quiz tests how well you remember the characters, factions, locations, technology and major events that shaped the series.

The questions span the story from the Ark and the first journey to Earth through the Grounder wars, Mount Weather, Praimfaya, Sanctum, Bardo and the final events of the series. Some questions focus on memorable characters and events, while others require you to remember smaller details that are easy to overlook.

Every question has four possible answers. A correct answer earns 2 points, giving you a maximum score of 100. Your final result places you into a rank ranging from Skaikru casualty to Pramheda.

Surviving After an Apocalypse

One of the central ideas behind The 100 is what happens when people have to rebuild society after civilization has collapsed. In a real disaster, survival would depend on much more than fighting ability. Access to clean water, food, shelter, medicine and reliable information would become critical very quickly.

Communities would also have to make difficult decisions about cooperation, leadership and the use of limited resources. A group that survives the first few weeks could still face enormous problems months or years later. Agriculture, sanitation, disease prevention, energy production and protection from environmental hazards would become increasingly important.

The hardest part of rebuilding would not simply be finding somewhere safe. It would be creating a stable system that could continue operating when supplies, technology and skilled people were limited.

Nuclear Apocalypse and Long-Term Survival

A nuclear disaster creates challenges that are very different from an ordinary emergency. Explosions and fires may cause immediate destruction, but radioactive contamination can create dangers that continue long after the initial event.

Fallout can contaminate soil, water and surfaces. Weather conditions can influence where radioactive material travels, making location and timing important factors in survival. People would need to understand the difference between immediate blast hazards and longer-term contamination.

Long-term survival would eventually depend on producing safe food and water, maintaining shelter, managing waste and rebuilding essential infrastructure. A community isolated from the original disaster might still face serious challenges if transport, electricity, medicine and communications disappeared.

Artificial Intelligence and Human Survival

Technology plays an important role in many post-apocalyptic stories because advanced systems can provide enormous advantages while also creating new risks. Artificial intelligence, automated infrastructure and computer-controlled systems could continue operating even when human society begins to fail.

AI could potentially help with navigation, scientific research, agriculture, energy management, communication and resource planning. However, dependence on complex technology also creates vulnerabilities. A system that people no longer understand or cannot repair could become difficult to control.

The real challenge is therefore not simply whether technology is powerful. It is whether humans can understand, maintain and safely control the systems they depend on. This question becomes especially important when technology is responsible for decisions affecting entire populations.

Space Colonies and Rebuilding Civilization

The 100 also explores an unusual survival problem: preserving humanity away from Earth. Living in space or on another world creates challenges that are very different from surviving on the surface of Earth.

A space settlement would need reliable life-support systems for oxygen, water, temperature control and waste management. Food production would become a major engineering problem, while equipment failures could become life-threatening because replacement parts and outside assistance might be unavailable.

Isolation would create another challenge. A small population would need to maintain technical skills, medical knowledge, education and social stability across generations. Rebuilding civilization is not simply about keeping people alive. It also means preserving knowledge and creating systems that allow future generations to survive independently.

The 100 Trivia and Frequently Asked Questions

How does The 100 Quiz work?

The quiz contains 50 multiple-choice questions. Each question has four possible answers, and you receive 2 points for every correct answer.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 100 points. Your score is converted into a rank based on the result system used by the quiz.

Does the quiz cover all seasons?

Yes. The questions cover material from throughout the series, including the Ark, Grounders, Mount Weather, Praimfaya, Sanctum, the Primes, Bardo and the final storyline.

Is this a survival quiz?

This is primarily a knowledge and trivia quiz. It uses the survival, science-fiction and post-apocalyptic themes of The 100, but your score measures how well you remember the series rather than whether you could actually survive an apocalypse.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. After receiving your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz and attempt a higher score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use the Challenge Your Friends option after completing the quiz to share your result and see whether another fan can beat your score.
